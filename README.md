# 🛒 বাজার দর (BazarDor)

### 🇧🇩 Your Daily Market Price Companion in Bangladesh

**Know the price. Track the change. Shop smarter!**

A modern and responsive web application that helps users explore daily market prices of essential products in Bangladesh. Compare prices, track market changes, and stay updated with everyday necessities.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 📊 Daily Market Prices
Explore the latest prices of essential products such as rice, vegetables, fish, meat, and other daily necessities.

### 2. 📈 Price Tracking
Identify products with increasing or decreasing prices through visual indicators and percentage badges.

### 3. 🔍 Product Details
View detailed product information, including minimum, maximum, and average prices across different local markets.

### 4. 📂 Category & Price Sorting
Browse products by category and sort them from lowest to highest or highest to lowest price.

### 5. 🔐 User Authentication & Profile
Register, sign in with email or social accounts, access protected pages, and update profile information.
